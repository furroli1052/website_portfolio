Main Idea

Create a portfolio website that would be my magnus opum in the status quo.

Process

Step 1

---------------------------------

In college, I was taught a basic HTML course.

------------------------------------------------------------------

I expanded on the html knowledge by transforming the playing field from 2d to 3d. To do this, I self taught myself how to use three.js a javascript library that enabled 3d objects to be animated in an html file.

---------------------------------

Step 2

---------------------------------

After I learned the basics of three.js I got free 3d models from the internet and decided to map out how I wanted my website to be like. I didn't want to do anything to crazy and with the little resource I had, I decided to go with a dark room with vintage TV's stacked on top of each other.

------------------------------------------------------------------

It sounded hard, but with the help of chatgpt and online tutorials, I slowly pieced together the website. bit by bit and making adjustments along the way. However, there was a problem, sure the models looked cool when they were stacked on top of each other, but there is a wow factor that truly elevates the scene.

------------------------------------------------------------------

Then I started browsing the internet for references and realized that I could add hacker vibes to round everything together and breathe life into my design

---------------------------------

Next Steps

---------------------------------

The problem now is that there are no special parts that make pages like this work.

------------------------------------------------------------------

My goal is to learn Blender currently and create scenes where I can make pages such as explaining the process to be interesting and full of style.

------------------------------------------------------------------

I want each field I'm interested to be a different world.
