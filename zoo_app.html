Main Idea

With the increased problem of obesity in the status quo,

our team aims to create a motivating way to combat this issue.

Our solution? Use the magic of Pokemon GO to motivate people to exercise.

Process

Problem

---------------------------------

In the first stage of this project, our team conducted user research through interviews and online surveys, developed user personas, and defined key user tasks.

------------------------------------------------------------------

These interviews helped us identify the main pain points of our target users, which we used to generate a prioritized list of app features.

---------------------------------

Solution

---------------------------------

The main pain point that was identified was the lack of motivation to exercise. We went deeper into this painpoint and discovered this could be solved with company, easy to learn exercises, busy schedules, and choice.

------------------------------------------------------------------

Our solution was through a system similar to pokemon GO. Through completing tasks, users would unlock pets and that would act as a reward system. To make it personalized, users are able to modify the pets so there can be some interactivity in online communities, solving the issue of company.

------------------------------------------------------------------

The rest of the pain points can be solved through easy to navigate UI and a simple onboarding process. We created low-fidelity wireframes to test the usability of our app and to get feedback from users.

---------------------------------

Result

---------------------------------

Based on the user feedback from our low fidelity wireframes, our team created high-fidelity wireframes and a prototype of the app using Figma.
